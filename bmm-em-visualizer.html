LOG-LIKELIHOOD, BY ITERATION
Each E-step / M-step pair can only raise this number (or leave it flat) — that's the EM guarantee. Flattening ≈ convergence: the μk prototypes have settled and further iterations won't reshape them much.
Reading the board: the E-step computes, for every sample, how plausible each prototype is as its source (that's γ) — those numbers feed the right-hand panel. The M-step then redraws each μk as the γ-weighted average of every sample, pulling each prototype toward the samples that currently "trust" it most. On real MNIST at K=20 with 784 pixels, this same loop tends to settle into roughly one-to-two prototypes per digit, plus a few components that pick up unusual writing styles or go unused.
